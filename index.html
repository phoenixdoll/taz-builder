Trait, profession, and cost data read from the installed game's vanilla scripts and the JOST / Somewhat Traits mod files. The point pool follows vanilla's real formula: Free Trait Points (a sandbox setting, "CharacterFreePoints") plus the chosen profession's own cost — professions add to or subtract from the pool directly, they aren't "spent" the way traits are. This omits the vanilla NegativeTraitsPenalty offset (a minor sandbox option limiting free points from negative traits when disabled — on by default). Somewhat Traits' effects (moodle/action changes) aren't modeled numerically here — only its point cost and mutual exclusions with vanilla, where the mods currently define any. If a server tunes JOST's sandbox numbers away from its defaults, the caps/levels shown here won't match.
